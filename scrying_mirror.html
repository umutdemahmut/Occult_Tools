ABOUT — SPECVLVM.EXE

Two oscillators drive a field of light behind black glass. Each one produces a sine, triangle, saw, square, pulse or sample-and-hold, laid out as parallel lines, concentric rings, a spiral or a grid. OSC B phase-modulates OSC A through X-MOD. WARP pushes the whole coordinate system through a turbulent noise field, which is what stops the pattern reading as stripes. SNOW crossfades the result into raw per-frame static.

MIRROR folds the field about a vertical axis. Weak bilateral symmetry is the strongest pareidolic cue there is, so this is the control that decides how readily faces arrive. SHADES quantises the tonal range; at two or three shades the glass stops making gradients and starts making regions, which the eye reads as edges of things.

Nothing is drawn here. There is no image underneath, so nothing can resolve. Whatever appears is assembled behind your eyes and belongs to you.

Before you gaze: this is high-contrast flickering light over a large area. If you are photosensitive or prone to migraine, switch on CALM, pull LUSTRE down, raise SHADES, and keep sessions short.
