About Sigil

Sigil.exe — version 11.0

Vowels and repeats are struck from the statement. What survives is set as real letterforms, each in its own face, stacked and merged into one silhouette. Where two letters share a stroke and sit on top of one another, the merge makes it one stroke.

Fusion searches each letter for the placement that shares most ink with the letters already down, so common stems and bowls collapse instead of accumulating. Shared strokes are easiest to find within a single face; mixed faces are built differently and fuse less.

The ground takes any image you supply. Fade, Contrast and Mono are the controls that keep it from swallowing the figure; Colour will fight it hardest. Tone is computed pixel by pixel rather than through canvas filters, which browsers support unevenly.

Fourteen faces, seven of them blackletter or medieval. Any .ttf, .otf or .woff you add is registered with the browser for this session only and never leaves your machine.

Solid mode exports faithfully to SVG as editable text, with the processed ground embedded. Outline and Clean are raster operations; save those as PNG. The 1904 Mathers & Crowley Goetia is public domain, so scans of the original plates drop straight in.
